# Prisoner Codes (Ciphers)

Watched a movie or TV where someone tapped on a surface or pipe to send morse code? Well morse uses Carrier Wave (CW) and requires a 1 count duration signal and a 3 count duration signal. A tap on a surface is instant, no duration. So regular morse code doesn't work in movies and TV or some real life situations.

Tap code (Polybius square): used by prisoners of war. Letters sit on a 5×5 grid (C and K share a cell) and are sent as a group of taps for the row, a pause, then a group for the column. It needs only tap count and pause length, with no dit/dah distinction.
